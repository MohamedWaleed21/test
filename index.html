Gauss’s law

∮ E · dA = Qenc / ε₀
E = |σ| / (2ε₀) For an ideal infinite uniformly charged non-conducting sheet, the field magnitude is the same on both sides.
2EA = |σ|A/ε₀

Flux passes through the two flat end caps because their area vectors are parallel to E (for +σ). On the curved side, dA is perpendicular to E, so its flux is zero. The pillbox encloses charge Qenc = σA.
